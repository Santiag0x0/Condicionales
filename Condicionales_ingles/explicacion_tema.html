🌟 Learn English Conditionals

🔁Zero Conditional

Use: To talk about facts, rules, or things that are always true.

Structure: If + present simple, present simple

If you mix red and blue, you get purple.

🌦️First Conditional

Use: To talk about real and possible situations in the future.

Structure: If + present simple, will + verb

If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic.

🌀Second Conditional

Use: To imagine situations that are not true or unlikely in the present or future.

Structure: If + past simple, would + verb

If I were a bird, I would fly around the world.

🛠️Common Variations

Sometimes, we use variations of "if" like:

  • Unless: Unless you study, you won’t pass.
  • As long as: You can play as long as you finish your homework.
  • Provided that: I’ll help you, provided that you ask politely.
